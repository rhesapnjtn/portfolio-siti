## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a high-fashion, print-editorial aesthetic engineered for an Information Systems graduate specializing in frontend development. The visual identity bridges runway-level art direction with precision web architecture: sharp typographic authority, structured magazine grids, generous negative space, and curated chromatic pops.

### Personality & Tone
- **Curated & Discerning:** Unapologetic restraint, allowing projects and code to read like featured exhibitions rather than a generic resume repository.
- **Intellectual & Technical:** Demonstrating systems thinking, pristine semantic structure, and deliberate typographic rhythm.
- **Expressive Edge:** Grounded in haute-couture publication layouts, accented by vibrant digital-first accents (electric lavender and royal cobalt) that convey modern interactive fluency.

### Visual Architecture
The style employs a hybrid of **High-Editorial Magazine Minimalism** and **Contemporary Digital Precision**. Layouts rely on asymmetric margins, monumental display type overlapping subtle structural boundaries, hairline borders, and warm tactile surfaces that eliminate cold corporate sterility.

## Colors

The palette establishes a physical publication feel through a warm, linen off-white canvas overlaid with deep carbon-charcoal ink, punctuated by digital-native jewel tones.

### Color Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#F8F6F3`):** Warm unbleached paper surface providing an organic, tactile reading foundation without visual fatigue.
- **Ink / Primary Typography (`#121316`):** High-density charcoal, softer than pure black, ensuring razor-sharp editorial legibility.
- **Primary Accent — Royal Cobalt (`#1D4ED8`):** Authoritative, vivid, and structural. Reserved for dominant actions, active navigational states, and key anchors.
- **Secondary Accent — Electric Lavender (`#8B5CF6`):** Kinetic and progressive. Used for badges, technical metadata markers, focus rings, and interactive flourishes.
- **Highlight Accent — Butter Yellow (`#FEF08A`):** Tactile highlighter marker tone. Applied deliberately to pill tags, callout pill backgrounds, and micro-attention callouts.
- **Subtle Surface & Lines (`#E6E2DC`):** Fine hairline grid boundaries, micro-separators, and card container strokes.

## Typography

The typography couples the architectural audacity of **Syne** with the frictionless clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans**, grounded by the technical precision of **Space Grotesk** for engineering annotations and metadata.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Guidelines
- **Hero & Headlines (`Syne`):** High-impact structural sans. Used for editorial mastheads, project titling, and numerical numbering (e.g., "01 / ARCHITECTURE"). Kerning should remain tight to preserve block-like visual strength.
- **Body & Longform (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Warm, contemporary geometric sans engineered for optical legibility at scale. Delivers balanced text-block density without drawing attention away from headlines.
- **Labels, Indexing & Tech Stacks (`Space Grotesk`):** Fixed-feel uppercase tracking applied to engineering metrics, date ranges, taxonomy pills, and interactive metadata.
- **Micro-Editorial Flourishes:** Project indices and section titles incorporate uppercase roman numerals or fractional indices (`VOL. 26`, `NO. 03`) set in `Space Grotesk` to mimic magazine folio notations.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an asymmetric 12-column editorial publishing grid that alternates between expansive full-bleed displays and compressed, disciplined text ribbons.

### Grid Rhythm & Composition
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column dynamic grid with `2.5rem` gutters and `4rem` outer canvas margins. Features intentional asymmetric placement—e.g., project descriptions occupying columns 1–5, with interactive previews occupying columns 6–12.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer gutters. Dense horizontal stacking with consistent bottom clearance to maintain vertical breathing room.

### Spacing Principles
- Use generous vertical spacing (`space-xl`) between distinct narrative chapters to mimic physical page turns.
- Internal component groupings stick to strict compact metrics (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) to enforce structural unity before letting elements breathe on the outer canvas.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects heavy skeuomorphic drops and heavy ambient shadows in favor of **Tonal Paper Layering** and **Crisp Structural Hairlines**.

### Structural Depth
- **Surface Elevation:** Tonal layering shifts from the base `#F8F6F3` to a clean pure white `#FFFFFF` on active cards, simulating sheets of paper stacked on an editor's desk.
- **Hairline Framing:** Micro-borders styled as `1px solid rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.08)` provide spatial definition without adding optical weight.
- **Editorial Hover Offsets:** Interactive elements elevate subtly using sharp, unblurred hard-edge micro-offsets: `2px 2px 0px #121316` instead of blurred diffusion.
- **Frosted Overlays:** Modal viewports and floating sticky headers utilize an ultra-subtle backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) combined with a `rgba(248, 246, 243, 0.85)` tint to preserve paper tactile richness beneath scrolling text.

## Shapes

The design system maintains an intentional, architectural crispness through **Soft (`roundedness: 1`)** geometry. 

- **Containers & Project Cards:** Structured with subtle `0.25rem` corners to retain a crisp, printed-card look without sharp digital aggression.
- **Chips, Category Tags & Meta Pills:** Shaped as full pills (`9999px`) to create an organic typographic contrast against rigid rectangular article containers and grid modules.
- **Interactive Buttons:** Minimal corner radii (`0.25rem` to `0.375rem`) anchoring a tailored, editorial identity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Issue Featured CTA):** Solid `#121316` background with `#F8F6F3` typography, `0.25rem` corner radius, `Space Grotesk` uppercase weight. On hover, transitions cleanly to `#1D4ED8` with a `2px 2px 0px #121316` hard shadow shift.
- **Secondary Action (Code View / Inspect):** Transparent fill with a `1px solid #121316` border. Inverts to `#121316` fill with white text upon hover.
- **Tertiary Link:** Clean underlined text in `Space Grotesk` with an offset hairline rule that expands rightward on hover.

### Chips & Tags
- **Technology Badges:** Full-pill silhouette with `#FEF08A` (Butter Yellow) background and `#121316` label text.
- **Status Pills (e.g., "Available for MagangHub 2026"):** Translucent `#8B5CF6` (12% opacity) background with solid `#8B5CF6` typography and a pulsing live status dot indicator.

### Project & Portfolio Cards
- **Editorial Exhibition Cards:** `#FFFFFF` canvas, `1px solid rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.08)` hairline border, wrapped in generous internal padding (`space-md` to `space-lg`).
- **Card Header:** Numbered index (`01`, `02`) in oversized `Syne` faded ink (`rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.2)`), followed by the project name and dynamic role tag.
- **Interactive State:** Border deepens to `#121316` with a micro-lift of `-2px` on the Y-axis.

### Input Fields & Contact Elements
- **Text Inputs:** Borderless top, left, and right; defined exclusively by a distinct `1.5px solid #121316` bottom baseline rule. Background remains transparent to inherit the `#F8F6F3` canvas tone.
- **Floating Labels:** `Space Grotesk` uppercase typography, shifting to `#1D4ED8` when focused.

### Micro-Components (Editorial Highlights)
- **Pull Quotes & Callouts:** Border-left of `3px solid #8B5CF6`, set in italicized `Plus Jakarta Sans` or display `Syne`, paired with Butter Yellow background tinting on key emphasized terms.
- **Engineering Spec Sheet (Quick Info Grid):** 2-column or 4-column structured key-value list bounded by top and bottom hairline borders, pairing uppercase `label-meta` keys with precise `body-md` values.
